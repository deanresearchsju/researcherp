# Security

## Reporting a problem

If you find a weakness, a leaked password or key, or personal data in a place it should not be, write to **deanresearch.sju@dmifoundations.org** with what you saw and where. Please do not share details publicly until the Research Office has replied.

## What to do at once if a key or password leaks

1. Change the leaked item (the steps are in [docs/SECURITY.md](docs/SECURITY.md), section "If a key leaks").
2. Tell the Dean of Research.
3. Read the audit trail in the ERP (Administration) for anything unusual.

The full security notes, including what is protected and what is still to be strengthened, are in [docs/SECURITY.md](docs/SECURITY.md).
