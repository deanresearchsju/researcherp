# Guide for a supervisor

## Signing in

Your sign-in opens when your recognition is approved; the login details are mailed to you. Set a new password at the first sign-in.

## What you see

- **Scholars**: _My scholars_, the scholars allotted to you, with their progress.
- **Approvals**: _Scholar requests_, _Doctoral committees_, _Synopsis and thesis_.
- **Supervision**: _RAC proposal_, _Thesis evaluators_, _Colloquium_.
- **Documents**: _Approved documents_, _Forms and formats_.

## Approving what your scholars send

Everything your scholars submit comes to you first.

1. Open the item under **Approvals**. Use **View files** to read the attachment.
2. **Approve** (with optional remarks): it goes to the Dean, and your name, date and time are stamped on the document.
3. **Reject** with comments (required): it goes back to the scholar, who corrects and submits again.

## The RAC

After your scholar is admitted, **RAC proposal**: name two internal and two external members with their designation, department, institution, e-mail, phone and specialisation. The Dean approves one internal and one external member. After that the RAC shows to you and to the scholar.

## Thesis evaluators (confidential)

After the Dean accepts the synopsis, **Thesis evaluators**: propose nine experts, three foreign, three Indian outside Tamil Nadu, three within Tamil Nadu. The Dean checks and forwards; the Vice Chancellor selects. You can correct your list until the Dean forwards it. The scholar never sees it.

## Your scholars and your vacancy

Your quota is fixed by your designation (Professor 8, Associate Professor 6, Assistant Professor 4). The office allots scholars only while you have a vacancy.

## My details

At your **first sign-in** you set a new password and are taken to **My details**: your phone, campus, area of research (short topics separated by commas), Vidwan ID and a biography of up to 200 words. They are saved at once. Your name, designation and department are the University's record: write to the research office to change them.

## Your public profile

The Research Office keeps your photograph, a biography of up to 200 words, your area of research and your Vidwan ID for the Supervisors page. Send them to the office to add or change.
