# Guide for the Research Office (staff and administrator)

What you see depends on the menus the administrator gave you. The administrator sees all.

## At the start of a session

1. **Administration, Fee settings**: check the fees.
2. **Website, Research areas**: the schools and departments an applicant can choose; add a department here if a supervisor names one that is missing.
3. **Registers, Supervisor register**: quota, status and department of every supervisor; the vacancies come from here.
4. **Admissions, Application window**: set the session and the last date and open applications.
5. Check the users and their rights (Administration, Users).

## Admissions

- **Applications**: verify fees (or Accounts does), look at each application.
- **Selection** (nine steps): Scrutiny, Shortlist, Entrance (hall tickets, seating, attendance Present or Absent), Marks, Merit list, Guide, Letters, Reporting, Finish. A step opens only after the one before. The application window must be closed before the shortlist is completed. Guide allotment offers only supervisors with a vacancy; an interdisciplinary candidate needs a co-supervisor. Letters are issued after the RAB or Vice Chancellor approval is recorded. In Reporting, tick each document; submitting the checklist opens the scholar's sign-in. **Finish** offers vacant seats to the waiting list, archives the session, starts the scholars in semester 1 and opens the next window.
- **Incomplete applications**: follow up applications that were started and not finished.
- **Admission archive** keeps closed sessions.

## Registers

- **Edit** is on every supervisor of the Supervisor register (including those recognised later). The administrator can also **import supervisors and scholars from Excel** (see [../DATA-UPLOAD.md](../DATA-UPLOAD.md)).

- **Scholar register** and **Scholar information**: Scholar information is where the scholar's details are entered and locked (only the administrator corrects). They change the register, the reports and the exports at once.
- **Registration numbers**: record the numbers the COE issues, batch by batch. A number is permanent.
- **Supervisor register**: the list of supervisors with quota, vacancy and status; mark inactive or resigned; edit details.

## Course work and examination

1. **Registration**: first add the subject code and title (Subjects); then open the window for the batch.
2. **Exam setup**: campuses with rooms and seats, the timetable, marks and grades, instructions.
3. **Hall tickets and seating**: issue hall tickets after Accounts approves; arrange seating; print the plan; mark attendance Present or Absent for each paper and campus and **Freeze and forward to COE**.
4. **Result approval** (administrator): record the passing board decision; **publish**.
5. **Semester promotion**: promote batches; a scholar is held for the reasons shown (course work, report, RAC, first committee, passed out or exit, annual fee) unless you tick Promote anyway.

## Invigilation duty

Under **Admissions, Entrance invigilation** (the entrance test) and **Course work, Invigilation duty** (the course-work examination):

1. **Invigilators**: add the people (one by one, paste a list from Excel, or add the supervisors from the register). Add the dates a person cannot attend.
2. **Duty plan**: set the number of candidates from which a room gets two invigilators, how many invigilators are on reserved duty in each sitting, the heading of the letter and the Chief Superintendent. The rooms come from the seating: arrange the seating (Selection, Entrance) or issue the hall tickets with seating (Course work) first. Press **Allot invigilators**; change any place from its list (the system refuses a person already on duty in that sitting or not available that day). Then **Finalise plan**.
3. **Letters**: after the plan is final, download one letter for each person or all in one PDF. The letters carry no signatures; the footer says they are system-generated.
4. **Date-wise list**: all duties by date and sitting in one running serial order, with the reserved invigilators at the end of each sitting; export to Excel or PDF.

Only the administrator can reopen a final plan. If seating changes after allotting, press **Allot again** (while the plan is a draft).

## Doctoral progress and requests

Committees, confirmation, colloquium, publications, six-month reports, plagiarism check (issue the similarity certificate), synopsis, thesis, examiner reports, viva, completion. Requests: supervisor change, mode change, title change, leave, extension, cancellation.

## Supervision

RAC proposals, supervisor transfer (only to a supervisor with a vacancy), supervisor exit.

## Fees

Fee collection and Fee approvals.

## Letters, reports and the website

Letters, Reports and downloads (lists in Excel and PDF), Forms and formats. Website: News and events, Helpdesk, Research areas, Our Team, Supervisor profiles.

## Taking care

- Take a backup before a step is reopened, a session is finished or data is cleared (see [BACKUP-AND-RESTORE.md](../BACKUP-AND-RESTORE.md)).
- Approve and reject with real comments.
- Nothing personal in chat or personal mail.
