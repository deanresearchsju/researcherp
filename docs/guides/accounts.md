# Guide for Accounts

You see five menus under **Fee verification**, each with **Pending** and **Approved** tabs:

| Menu                         | The fee                                             |
| ---------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------- |
| Registration fee             | The Ph.D. application fee paid with the application |
| Course-work registration fee | The fee to register for the course-work examination |
| Annual fee                   | The annual research (tuition) fee                   |
| Synopsis fee                 | The fee to submit the synopsis                      |
| Thesis fee                   | The fee to submit the thesis                        |

## Checking a payment

1. Open **Pending** and press **Review receipt**. The receipt is shown with the payment details.
2. **Approve payment**: the step that needed the fee opens for the scholar.
3. Or **Reject** with a comment (required): the scholar sees it and pays again with the right receipt.

## Looking back

Filter by year or by from and to date, search, and **Export** to PDF or Excel (the export carries the dates). Every year stays available.

## Remember

- The fee amounts are set by the administrator (Administration, Fee settings); a fee already paid never changes.
- A part payment can be approved; the scholar's balance then shows what is left.
