# Guide for a scholar

## Signing in

Your sign-in is your **e-mail**. It opens when the Research Office has checked your documents and submitted your admission checklist; a first password is mailed to you. Set a new password when asked.

## What you see

**Dashboard**, then four groups:

- **Course work**: Registration, Hall ticket, Results.
- **Doctoral progress**: RAC members, First committee, Ph.D. confirmation, Colloquium, Publications, Six-month report, Third committee, Plagiarism check, Synopsis, Thesis, Viva, Completion.
- **Requests**: Supervisor change, Full-time to part-time, Title change, Leave, Extension, Ph.D. cancellation.
- **Scholar records**: ABC ID, Fee, Approved documents, Forms and formats.

## Course work

1. **Registration**: when your batch's window is open, choose the papers from the list (up to two new papers a semester, and any paper you must write again), choose the **campus** where you will write, upload the fee receipt, and submit.
2. Accounts approves the fee. If it is rejected you see the comment; correct it and send again.
3. **Hall ticket**: after the fee is approved the office issues it. Download it; it shows the papers, campus, room and seat.
4. **Results**: after the passing board and publication you see the marks, grade and Pass, RA or AAA for each paper.

## Fees

Under **Fee** pay the annual fee (and the synopsis and thesis fees when they are fixed) and upload each receipt. A step that needs a fee opens only after Accounts approves the payment.

## Sending something to the University

Every submission and request (a committee report, a six-month report, the synopsis, the thesis, a leave or extension request, a change of supervisor or title) goes to your **supervisor first, then to the Dean**.

1. Open the menu, fill in, attach the file (PDF, JPG or PNG, at most 2 MB), submit.
2. The status shows under the item: _Submitted to Supervisor_, _Approved by Supervisor_ (then _Submitted to Dean_), _Approved by Dean_, or _Rejected by Supervisor_ or _Rejected by Dean_ with the comments.
3. If rejected, correct and submit again.
4. When approved, **Approved documents** gives the stamped copy with the names, dates and times.

## The similarity check

Before the synopsis and before the thesis the Research Office checks your work with the DrillBit software and issues a certificate. You can submit the synopsis or the thesis only after it is issued.

## Your supervisor and your RAC

Your guide's details appear after your first payment is approved. **RAC members** shows the committee once the Dean has approved it. You do not see the examiners of your thesis; that is confidential.

## Your details

At your **first sign-in** you set a new password and are then taken to **My details**: check what is there (from your application or the University's list) and complete what is missing. Use the same boxes later to correct your mobile, address or parents' details. What you send goes to the Research Office to confirm. A box takes only what it should: dates are chosen from a calendar, numbers take digits only, and names take letters only.

The Research Office keeps your personal details. If something is wrong (name, mobile, address), write to the office to have it corrected; it then changes everywhere.
