# Guide for the Dean

You give the final approval. The administrator gives you the menus you need.

## What reaches you

Everything a scholar submits comes to you **after the supervisor has approved it**: committee reports, six-month reports, synopsis, thesis, supervisor change, mode change, title change, leave, extension, cancellation, ABC ID. You cannot approve before the supervisor has.

1. Open the item (for example under **Requests** or **Doctoral progress**). **View files** shows the attachment with the supervisor's approval stamped on it.
2. **Approve** (your name, date and time are stamped) or **Reject with comments** (required). A rejected item returns to the scholar.

## The RAC

Under **Supervision, RAC**: the supervisor names two internal and two external members; you approve one internal and one external. It then shows to the supervisor and the scholar.

## The thesis evaluators (confidential)

Under **Doctoral progress, Thesis evaluators**: check the nominees' specialisation against the thesis domain and **Check and forward to the Vice Chancellor**; after the Vice Chancellor's choice record the invitations (invited, accepted, declined). **Reopen** returns the list to the supervisor. The sheet shows the three steps with date and time.

## Guide history

If you were given the menu, under **Supervision, Scholar allotment** you see the history of every allotment, change and transfer of a guide. Only you and the administrator see it.

## The picture of the programme

Registers, Reports and downloads and the Dashboard show progress by batch, supervisor and stage.

## Taking care

Approve and reject with real comments. Check the list of users and rights with the administrator at the start of each session.
