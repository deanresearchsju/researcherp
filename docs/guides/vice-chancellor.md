# Guide for the Vice Chancellor

You see three menus.

## Scholar register and Supervisor register

Read only. The Scholar register shows each scholar's registration number, department, batch, mode, guide and stage; **Details** gives the rest. The Supervisor register shows each supervisor's designation, department, area of research, quota, vacancy and status. Lists can be exported to Excel or PDF.

## Thesis evaluators (confidential)

After the supervisor has proposed nine evaluators (three foreign, three Indian outside Tamil Nadu, three within Tamil Nadu) and the Dean has checked and forwarded them, you choose the **first, second and third choice in each of the three groups**. The Dean then invites them in order. The scholar never sees the panel. Your name, date and time are recorded with your choice.

## The approval of the selection list

The provisional selection list is approved by the Research Advisory Board or by you; the office records the approval (body, reference, date) before letters are issued.

## RAC approval

Open **RAC approval**. The Dean has selected the external member; the nomination shows all members with their address, contact, area of research and latest five publications. **Approve the RAC**, or **Return to supervisor** with remarks (required). The RAC is in force, and shown to the scholar and the supervisor, only after your approval.
