# Guide for the Controller of Examinations: the two sign-ins

The administrator creates two kinds of sign-in.

## Marks entry

Menu **Marks entry**. Any number of such sign-ins can work at the same time, on different papers or campuses; nothing is lost if two save together.

1. Choose the batch and semester, the paper (or all), the campus.
2. A scholar's box opens only when the **attendance of that paper at that campus has been forwarded** by the office ("Attendance awaited" until then).
3. Type the marks (0 to the maximum, in steps of 0.5) and press Enter to move on. A scholar marked Absent becomes **AAA**; no marks are needed.
4. **Save marks**, then **Send for verification** when every scholar has marks or is absent. The paper is then locked.

## Verification

Menu **Verify and freeze marks**.

1. Check each paper that was sent. **Send back** with a note, or **Freeze**: a frozen mark can be changed by nobody.
2. Print the marks sheets to sign and keep.
3. When every paper is frozen, **Submit to the research office**.
4. If the passing board asks for changes (for example bossing), the chosen papers return to marks entry with the board's note; correct, freeze again and submit the final file.

## After that

The office records the board's decision and publishes: the scholars see marks, grade and Pass, RA or AAA. A published result cannot be changed.

## Registration numbers

The Controller of Examinations issues each scholar's permanent registration number; the Research Office records it.
